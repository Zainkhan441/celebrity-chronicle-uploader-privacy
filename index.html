Privacy Policy — Celebrity Chronicle Uploader

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Celebrity Chronicle Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Celebrity Chronicle YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public and it has no other users.

What the app accesses

The app connects to the channel owner's Google account using Google OAuth, with only these permissions:

  • Upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload scope)
  • Manage the channel's YouTube videos (youtube scope)

These permissions are used only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and scheduled publish times on the Celebrity Chronicle channel.

What data is stored

The app stores its OAuth access and refresh tokens locally on the operator's own computer, only so it can upload videos without signing in every time. No other personal data is collected or stored.

What data is shared

Nothing. The app does not share, sell, or send any data to any third party. It does not collect information about viewers, and it does not use analytics or advertising.

Data removal

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google account permissions page. Revoking access immediately stops the app from reaching the YouTube account.
